Tailoring takes forever.

The Solution

Your personal AI career advisor — built for serious job seekers.

CareerPocket AI is more than a job tracker. It's a private AI-powered career advisor that understands your background, your real work experience, and your career aspirations — and puts that knowledge to work on every single application.

Start by building your career profile. CareerPocket AI learns who you are, what you've done, and where you want to go. When you find a role, it scores the fit against your aspirations and your profile — so you make informed decisions about where to invest your effort.

When you decide to apply, it drafts a tailored cover letter grounded strictly in your real experience and the specific job description. Then it generates an ATS-optimised tailored resume — reordered, resharpened, and formatted to pass AI screening systems — without ever touching your master copy.

After you submit, CareerPocket AI remembers. Every cover letter and tailored resume is saved against its job, viewable any time. As you progress through rounds, add notes — what was discussed, what gaps came up, what to prepare for next.

  • Generates an ATS-optimised resume per application — without touching your master copy
  • Saves every document against its job — you always know exactly what you sent
  • Captures interview notes per application and uses them to sharpen the next round
  • Gives you an AI advisor that knows your full history and keeps getting smarter
