Cybersecurity Governance & Certification

Strong governance isn't a blocker to innovation — it's what makes fast, confident innovation possible. Trusted-Aide provides right-sized cybersecurity governance and certification support for organisations at every stage, and is the foundation that makes safe AI and digital innovation possible.

ISO 27001 Implementation & Audit Preparation

Scope definition, risk assessment, controls, documentation, and internal audit support.

SOC 2 Readiness

CyberTrust & Cyber Essentials Readiness

Singapore-specific frameworks for SMEs needing customer assurance.

MLPS 2.0 Certification (China)

Achieve compliance with China's Multi-Level Protection Scheme (MLPS 2.0), a mandatory cybersecurity framework for operating digital systems in China.

We guide you through classification, gap assessment, control implementation, documentation, and coordination with accredited Chinese assessors to ensure your systems meet MLPS requirements for secure operations within China's regulatory environment.

PDPA Compliance & Privacy Governance
