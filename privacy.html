Privacy Policy

How Trusted-Aide handles enquiry details, contact information, and communication consent.

Trusted-Aide respects your privacy. This policy explains how we use information submitted through our contact form or provided during business discussions.

Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, work email, company or organisation, country, area of interest, timeline, message, and any other details you choose to provide.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, understand your organisation's needs, arrange follow-up discussions, and provide relevant advisory or service information.

If you choose to opt in, we may occasionally send relevant updates, insights, or marketing materials. You may opt out of these communications at any time.

No Resale of Contact Information

Trusted-Aide does not resell your contact information. We do not sell enquiry details, contact lists, or personal information to third-party marketers.

Sharing and Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers, such as form processing or email tools, to receive and manage enquiries. These providers process information only as needed to support our business operations.

Retention and Opt-Out

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you, manage business relationships, and maintain appropriate records. You may ask us to update or remove your contact details, or to stop sending marketing communications.

Contact

For privacy questions or opt-out requests, please contact us through the Trusted-Aide contact form.
