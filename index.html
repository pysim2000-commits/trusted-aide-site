Application Security

Identify Application Vulnerabilities, Cut False Positives, Speed Remediation Workflows

Modern applications are prime targets for attackers. Yet most organisations face an overwhelming volume of scanner findings, high false-positive rates, and slow remediation cycles that leave real risks exposed for longer than necessary.

Trusted-Aide helps organisations discover real vulnerabilities earlier, reduce time spent on noise and false positives, prioritise what matters most, and accelerate remediation support through developer-friendly workflows — without overclaiming what automation alone can achieve.
