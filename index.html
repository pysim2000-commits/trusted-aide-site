Featured Solution

Application Security

Identify Application Vulnerabilities, Cut False Positives, Speed Remediation Workflows

As cloud vendors have largely addressed infrastructure-level threats, application vulnerabilities have become the dominant and most neglected attack surface. Yet most organisations still face an overwhelming volume of scanner findings, high false-positive rates, and slow remediation cycles that leave real risks exposed for longer than necessary.

Trusted-Aide helps organisations discover real vulnerabilities earlier, reduce time spent on noise and false positives, prioritise what matters most, and accelerate remediation support through automated and developer-friendly workflows.
