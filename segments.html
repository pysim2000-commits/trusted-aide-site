Who Trusted-Aide Helps

We work with business leaders across four distinct customer groups. Each group faces different technology challenges — and we tailor our advisory and services accordingly.

Investors & Portfolio Leaders

Technology risk is a growing concern for investors. Whether you are evaluating a potential acquisition, managing a portfolio of companies, or trying to understand the technology health of an existing holding, Trusted-Aide provides independent advisory that translates technical complexity into clear business risk.

We help investment teams move quickly and confidently — with assessments calibrated to deal timelines and management styles.

How we help investors:

Key Technology Concerns Before Acquisition (Pre‑Deal)

  • Technology credibility check: Validate whether the target’s technology claims, architecture, and scalability are accurate and sustainable
  • Cybersecurity posture: Identify vulnerabilities, exposure, and compliance gaps that could create hidden liabilities
  • Technology debt and remediation cost: Estimate the investment required to bring systems to a secure, maintainable baseline
  • Licensing and IP validation: Confirm ownership, third‑party dependencies, and any restrictions that could impact deal value
  • Team capability and governance maturity: Assess whether the technical leadership and processes can support future growth
  • Integration feasibility: Evaluate how easily the target’s technology can align with existing portfolio systems

Key Technology Priorities After Acquisition (Post‑Deal)

  • Integration planning: Align technology, data, and cybersecurity frameworks across the portfolio
  • Cybersecurity uplift: Establish a consistent baseline and remediation roadmap
  • Governance and oversight: Implement frameworks for accountability, privacy, and AI ethics
  • Performance monitoring: Track technology health and ROI across portfolio companies
  • Fractional leadership support: Provide CIO/CISO‑level guidance to strengthen technology management and risk visibility

Multinationals in China & APAC

Operating across China and APAC brings regulatory complexity that most global technology teams are not fully prepared for. Data localisation, cybersecurity law compliance, cloud provider restrictions, and ecosystem differences all affect how technology should be architected and governed.

Trusted-Aide provides the regional knowledge and practical guidance that helps multinational organisations operate with confidence in APAC — without compromising global governance standards.

China Operations

APAC Governance

Cross-Border Advisory

Local Ecosystem Fit
