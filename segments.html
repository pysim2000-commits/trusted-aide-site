Key Technology Concerns Before Acquisition (Pre‑Deal)

  • Technology credibility check: Validate whether the target's technology claims, architecture, and scalability are accurate and sustainable
  • Cybersecurity posture: Identify vulnerabilities, exposure, and compliance gaps that could create hidden liabilities
  • Technology debt and remediation cost: Estimate the investment required to bring systems to a secure, maintainable baseline
  • Licensing and IP validation: Confirm ownership, third-party dependencies, and any restrictions that could impact deal value
  • Team capability and governance maturity: Assess whether the technical leadership and processes can support future growth
  • Integration feasibility: Evaluate how easily the target's technology can align with existing portfolio systems

Key Technology Priorities After Acquisition (Post‑Deal)

  • Integration planning: Align technology, data, and cybersecurity frameworks across the portfolio
  • Cybersecurity uplift: Establish a consistent security baseline and remediation roadmap
  • Governance and oversight: Implement frameworks for accountability, privacy, and AI ethics
  • Performance monitoring: Track technology health and ROI across portfolio companies
  • Fractional leadership support: Provide CIO/CISO‑level guidance to strengthen technology management and risk visibility

Global MNCs Operating Across China & APAC

Operating across China and APAC introduces regulatory and operational complexity that global technology teams often underestimate.

Key challenges include:

  • Data localisation and cross‑border data transfer restrictions
  • Compliance with China's CSL, PIPL, MLPS, and emerging AI regulations as well as regional regulations like PDPA etc
  • Cloud provider limitations and fragmented local ecosystems
  • Misalignment between global governance standards and local operational realities
  • Difficulty validating whether global solutions fit China/APAC environments

These issues can delay market entry, increase compliance risk, and create costly integration surprises.

China & Regional Companies Going International

Expanding into global markets brings a different set of technology and governance challenges.

Key concerns include:

  • Meeting international expectations for cybersecurity, privacy, and auditability
  • Aligning local technology and data practices with global compliance frameworks (GDPR, ISO, SOC2, etc.)
  • Validating China/APAC‑built solutions against global interoperability and security standards
  • Managing cross‑border data flows and vendor dependencies
  • Building governance maturity that supports international partnerships and investor confidence

Without the right guidance, these gaps can limit global expansion, slow partnerships, and expose hidden risks during due diligence.
